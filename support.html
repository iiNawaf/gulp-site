Support

Need help with Gulp! Candy Hole? Email nawafkatt@gmail.com and include your device model and OS version. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I play? Drag anywhere to move the hole. Swallow treats smaller than the hole to grow, then eat bigger ones. Clear the goals shown at the top before the timer ends.

How do I restore purchases? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Non-consumable items (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, No Ads Bundle) restore to the same Apple Account or Google account.

Do I need an account? No. There is no login; progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or by Google Play at Google Play Help.
