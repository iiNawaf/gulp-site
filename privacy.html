Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Gulp! Candy Hole ("the app"), published by Devology for Information Technology ("we"), handles information on iOS and Android.

No accounts, data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your progress, stars, coins, boosters and purchases are saved only on your device (PlayerPrefs). We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads)

The app shows rewarded ads (that you choose to watch for a reward) and interstitial ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies. There are no banner ads. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, these SDKs may collect:

  • device identifiers: on iOS the identifier for vendors (IDFV); on Android the Google advertising ID and app set ID. The app does not ask for iOS tracking permission and does not use the IDFA for cross-app tracking;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type and IP address (used to infer a coarse region);
  • ad interaction data such as ad impressions, clicks and ad-related app events, plus diagnostics such as crash and performance data from the SDK.

Unity's processing is governed by the Unity Privacy Policy. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. The app receives only a transaction record from the store to deliver and restore your items; it is kept on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Data collected by the advertising SDK is handled by Unity under its policy and retention rules.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier, or delete the app to remove all locally stored game data. For privacy requests, contact us below.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
